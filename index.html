How does it work?

You fill in a spreadsheet. The site makes itself.

  1. Tell me your names

    Send me the names of the couple. I create the shell of your website and give you the Google Sheet template.

  2. Get a quick walkthrough

    The sheet has all the instructions you need. I’ll also hop on a call and take you through the basics.

  3. Add your content

    Fill everything into the sheet. Upload images to Google Drive, make them accessible to anyone with the link, and paste that link into the sheet.

  4. Your site is live

    The site builds itself from what’s in the sheet. Change the sheet, and the site follows.
